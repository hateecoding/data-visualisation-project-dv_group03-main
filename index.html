Crash Timing - When and Where Crashes Peak

WHEN and WHERE do we act?

Focusing on the highest-risk State (from Chart 1 and 4), this chart pinpoints the precise Month/Day and Road Type where fatalities cluster. This provides the final, actionable intelligence required to deploy resources at the optimal time and location.

Key Insights:

• Morning Rush (6-9 AM): High risk on Highways during commute
• Evening Rush (15-21): Highest fatality periods on Arterial Roads
• Late Night (21-3): Significant risk on Local Roads
• Weekends: Outer rings show more late-night incidents

Chart Guide: 7 rings (Monday inner, Sunday outer) × 8 time periods. Darker red = higher fatalities.

How to Use:Use year slider to filter. Click filters to expand options. Hover cells for details.

Data Source: Australian Road Deaths Database (ARDD)
